From the stable to the shore

Around 2–3 hours · weather and horse welfare apply
  1. 01

    Arrive at the stable

    Meet the team and learn about the horses, their temperament and the riding experience.

  2. 02

    Safety briefing and practice

    Receive instructions and basic guidance from experienced staff. Follow the instructor’s directions throughout the ride.

  3. 03

    Guided horse ride

    Ride through suitable open areas and enjoy the surrounding scenery at a pace appropriate to the group’s experience.

  4. 04

    Sea experience

    If sea, weather and horse conditions are suitable, enjoy the planned horse-and-sea experience under staff supervision. Swimming is not guaranteed in unsafe conditions.

  5. 05

    Return to the stable

    Return the horses to the stable and conclude the experience with the team.
