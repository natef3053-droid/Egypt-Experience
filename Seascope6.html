See the Red Sea from below

Around 3 hours · marine conditions may affect the route
  1. 01

    Transfer to the departure point

    Travel from your hotel to the departure marina in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle.

  2. 02

    Board the Royal Seascope

    Meet the crew and receive instructions for moving safely between the upper deck and the underwater viewing area.

  3. 03

    Cruise over the reef

    Enjoy views from the upper deck as the boat travels across the Red Sea.

  4. 04

    Underwater viewing session

    Descend to the vessel’s enclosed viewing area to observe colourful fish, coral formations and underwater life without entering the water or scuba diving.

  5. 05

    Return to shore

    After the viewing experience, return to the departure point and take your transfer back to the agreed location.
