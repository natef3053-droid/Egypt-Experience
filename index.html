Local Tips

Little things worth knowing

Keep Some Cash

While cards work in major areas, smaller shops and markets prefer cash. Carry small Egyptian pound notes for daily purchases.

Respectful Photography

Follow site regulations regarding cameras, and always ask permission before taking photos of locals.

Protect Heritage

Egypt’s ancient monuments are unique. Please refrain from touching or scratching surfaces, climbing ancient structures, or sitting on fragile remains.

Stay Hydrated

Egypt can be hot and sunny. Keep drinking water and rest when needed during tours.

Explore Markets

Take time to explore local bazaars, and ask your guide about genuine crafts and traditions.

Ask Your Guide

Feel free to consult your guide anytime—they are here to help you understand the culture and enjoy your trip.

Good to Know

A few important reminders

Historical Sites

Wear comfortable shoes, protect yourself from the sun, and respect the monuments and the rules of each archaeological site. Please never touch, climb on or damage ancient structures.

Religious Places

Modest clothing is recommended when visiting churches, mosques and other places of worship. Keep a scarf or light cover available when needed.

Photography

Personal photography is allowed in many places, but some museums and archaeological areas have specific restrictions. Always follow signs and instructions from site staff.

Desert Trips

For desert adventures and overnight camping, always follow your guide’s instructions and the safety rules of your tour. Stay with your group and never leave the designated route or area on your own.

Red Sea Activities

The Red Sea is famous for its coral reefs and marine life. During snorkeling, boat and other water activities, follow the crew’s instructions, respect the marine environment, and do not touch coral or marine life.

Adventure Activities

For horseback riding, quad biking, boat activities and other adventures, listen to your guide, follow the activity rules, and choose activities that match your comfort and experience level.

One Last Tip

A gentle reminder

Immerse Yourself

Don’t try to see everything through your camera. Put it down sometimes. Look around. Enjoy Egypt.
