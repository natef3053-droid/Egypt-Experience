1. Privacy Policy

We use your information to answer enquiries and arrange your trip.

Egypt Experience, based in Hurghada, Egypt, handles the information you share when you contact us or request a tour, transfer, private trip, group tour, or itinerary.

How we use and share information

  • To respond to enquiries and confirm trip details, dates, guest numbers, preferences, and pickup arrangements.
  • To manage bookings, maintain necessary records, and protect our services from misuse.

We do not sell or rent personal information. Access is limited to people who need relevant details to arrange your service. Guides or translators are not routinely given your personal contact details; necessary operational details, such as pickup point, date, or guest count, may be shared when required.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, WhatsApp processes your messages and phone number under its own privacy terms. We retain booking information only as reasonably needed for the booking, records, disputes, or legal obligations, and take practical steps to protect it. Electronic communication cannot be guaranteed completely secure.

You may contact us to request access to or correction or deletion of your information. Some records may need to be kept where required by law.

2. Information We Handle

We ask for details needed to plan and coordinate your trip.

Depending on your request, the tour form or conversation may include:

  • Name and phone number or WhatsApp contact.
  • Requested date, number of guests, and travel preferences.
  • Hotel name or pickup address, plus relevant requests you choose to share.

Please do not send passport, payment-card, password, or other sensitive details unless we explain why they are necessary and agree a suitable method. Our usual enquiry process does not require card details.

The tour enquiry form opens WhatsApp. Check your message and share only the information needed to arrange your trip.

3. Cookies & Website Tools

External website resources may process basic technical data.

Your browser may process technical information needed to display the website. External resources, such as fonts and icon libraries, may receive connection data when loaded.

The website code reviewed for this policy does not intentionally configure analytics or advertising tracking tools. If such tools are added, this policy will be updated and any legally required consent choices will be provided.

You can manage cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect website features. WhatsApp and other external services follow their own privacy notices.

4. Terms of Service

Key terms for using the website and requesting travel services.

Egypt Experience provides information about tours, private and group trips, airport transfers, and multi-day itineraries in Egypt. The exact itinerary, inclusions, timing, availability, and price must be confirmed directly for each request.

Submitting a form or WhatsApp message is an enquiry, not a confirmed booking. A booking is confirmed when the service, date, guest count, pickup details, price, and payment method have been agreed with you.

Weather, safety, availability, venue rules, or circumstances outside reasonable control may require changes. We will communicate material changes and discuss practical alternatives where possible. Independent providers may have their own operating rules.

Unless stated otherwise, website text, branding, and original design elements may not be reproduced commercially without permission. Use the website lawfully for personal travel research and enquiries.

5. Bookings & Payments

Booking details and payment arrangements are confirmed directly.

Please provide accurate contact details, date, guest count, preferences, and pickup location so we can arrange your experience.

Depending on the agreed arrangement, payment may be made by bank transfer, mobile wallet, or cash, either shortly before the trip or after the service. Confirm the price, currency, inclusions, payment timing, and method with us before committing. Only pay to an account or number confirmed by Egypt Experience.

6. Cancellations & Changes

There is currently no standard cancellation fee.

Contact us through the WhatsApp conversation used to arrange your trip if you need to cancel or change a booking. Egypt Experience does not currently charge a standard guest cancellation fee.

Payment is normally arranged close to the trip or after the service, as agreed. If an exceptional booking involves advance payment or a third-party charge, applicable refund or cancellation conditions should be explained and agreed before payment.

If we cannot provide a confirmed service as agreed, we will contact you to discuss available alternatives or an appropriate resolution. Ask us to confirm any special arrangements in writing.

7. Guest Responsibilities

Help us keep your experience smooth and safe.

  • Provide accurate booking and pickup details, and tell us about changes promptly.
  • Be ready at the agreed pickup point and time.
  • Follow reasonable safety instructions from tour staff, drivers, venues, and authorities.
  • Respect local laws, communities, religious places, wildlife, and historical sites.
  • Tell us in advance about practical needs that may affect arrangements, if you wish.

These terms do not limit any rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.
